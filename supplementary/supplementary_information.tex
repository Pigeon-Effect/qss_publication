\section{Fractional citation shares by cluster}

Impact is the \textit{fractional citation sum}: a work's incoming citations are
split among the countries that produced it, in proportion to the institutional
shares recorded in \texttt{country\_of\_origin}, then summed within a cluster and
grouped into blocs. China is \texttt{CN + HK + MO + TW}; the EU-27 is the 27
member states; RoW is every remaining country.

Shares are read \textbf{across a row}: the four bloc columns of a cluster sum to
100\,\%. The \textit{\% corpus} column instead reads down --- it is that
cluster's share of all fractional citations in the corpus, and the column sums
to 100\,\% within each table. Meso- and macro-level figures are aggregations of
the micro-level citation sums, not averages of micro-level shares, so a
cluster's share is always its own citations over its own total.

\section{Document-intrusion validation}

Each trial shows the judge a panel of five documents --- four drawn from a
target cluster, one intruder drawn from a different cluster at the same level,
in randomised order --- with titles and abstracts truncated to 200 words, and
nothing else: no cluster label, no TF-IDF terms, no indication of which
document is which. The judge names the intruder. The random-guess baseline is
20\,\%.

Table~\ref{tab:s4} gives the three reported runs. \textit{Recovered} counts
responses whose verdict was read out of the reasoning trace because the model
never wrote the requested final line; \textit{forced choice} counts responses
that hit the token ceiling mid-reasoning and were resolved by presenting the
truncated trace to a second, non-reasoning model for extraction only. Neither
step guesses: no trial was scored by fallback to a random answer, and no
response went unparsed.

Accuracy rises monotonically as the taxonomy becomes finer, which is what the
protocol predicts: intrusion detection depends on the ratio of within-cluster
spread to between-cluster separation, and a macro-domain is heterogeneous by
construction. A panel drawn from \texttt{Natural Science} may pair structural
health monitoring with ionospheric prediction and rural land-use change; an
intruder is far less conspicuous there than among the narrow, lexically
distinctive research fronts at h3.

The complete record of all 3{,}000 trials --- the panel shown, the model's full
reasoning, the extracted verdict and the rule that extracted it --- is deposited
under \texttt{results/intrusion/}, one manifest (\texttt{.json}), one record per
trial (\texttt{.jsonl}) and one readable transcript (\texttt{.txt}) per level.
